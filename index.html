Identity architecture

Your public profile should never be your private identity.

Eden RealID is intended to be a shared account handle across Light of Hope, EdenNetwork, and PwnZ. Hope Labs is researching privacy-preserving uniqueness proofs, recovery, and non-linkable evidence. A public timestamp does not establish that an individual is unique.

Your Eden voice · Crypto and technology proposals

Suggest technology ideas. Vote together. Guide future DAO decisions.

Existing Eden RealID members may create advisory community polls on technology, tokens, security and network development when the poll service is enabled. Admins review suggestions and are the only people authorized to open official DAO consultations. The proposed one-human/EDEN-holder voting system is still research, not live voting or a promise of token rewards.

01 · Member-led ideas

Any eligible Eden RealID member may create a discussion poll and vote once per poll through the authenticated member service, subject to release readiness.

02 · Community attention

Popular polls help highlight member priorities but never automatically pass a DAO change, trigger spending or modify contracts.

03 · Admin governance

Only authorized Eden admins may open formal governance consultations. Future ZK and EDEN-holder ballots require independent security audits and launch approval.

Does EdenNetwork require a wallet?

No. Optional wallet features should not prevent ordinary community membership or participation.

Does a timestamp prove one person has only one account?

No. A timestamp records an event. Preventing duplicate issuance requires a separate, carefully evaluated uniqueness and enrollment design.

Can ANNEAL determine who gets access or votes?

No. ANNEAL is a supporting research and analysis layer only; trusted human and audited protocol controls determine access and governance outcomes.
