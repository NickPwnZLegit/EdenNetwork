Community polls & admin DAO consultations

EdenNetwork is a home for blockchain, cryptocurrency, developers, security, privacy research and technology governance. You can participate here without posting on Light of Hope or PwnZ. Your existing Eden RealID and current ecosystem agreement are required for member actions.

Community polls

Any eligible Eden RealID member can ask a question, choose voting options, and get other members' feedback. Polls are nonbinding and can draw admin attention.

Admin review

Admins may review popular member ideas, document their response, or decline a suggestion. High vote counts never automatically advance an idea.

Official DAO consultations

Only authorized admins may open formal governance consultations. These are separate from community polls and do not execute contracts or change the network.
