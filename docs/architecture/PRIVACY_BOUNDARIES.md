# Eden RealID | Public architecture and privacy boundaries

**Status: research design, not a deployed unique-human service.**

## Purpose
The future shared Eden RealID registry is intended to support Light of Hope, EdenNetwork, and PwnZ. A member's chosen public name is not the same as a verified unique-human credential. Account authentication is separate from a personhood/uniqueness proof.

## Public and private separation
- Public chain or audit commitments must not publish legal identity, biometrics, member account ownership, wallet linkage, phone/device identifiers, or reusable cross-application correlators.
- Authentication and account recovery records must remain in access-controlled systems, detached from Hope Labs' synthetic-only research and any public audit view.
- A public timestamp establishes evidence about a recorded event, **not** that two enrollments are from different humans.
- ZK membership proofs and scoped nullifiers can restrict reuse of an issued credential; preventing repeat issuance to one human requires a separately evaluated, consent-based enrollment method.
- Enrollment must support secure recovery, revocation, accessibility, appeals, and non-linkability evaluation.

## Governance and integrations
Proposals, eligibility, and votes need independently validated, privacy-preserving rules. Any token-holder supplementary vote is a governance policy decision and must not bypass the one-human eligibility system. Optional LightVault wallet access and optional Pi marketplace access must never become required for ordinary social participation.

Hope Guard and ANNEAL may consume reviewed, bounded aggregate research outputs only; neither makes identity, security, financial, or governance decisions.
